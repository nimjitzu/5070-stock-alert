Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

5070 Stock Alert is a personal stock-monitoring and notification service. It monitors product availability and can send SMS notifications when a configured product becomes available for purchase.

Information Collected

5070 Stock Alert uses the mobile phone number voluntarily provided by the account owner for the purpose of delivering requested SMS stock-availability notifications.

How Information Is Used

The mobile phone number is used solely to deliver SMS notifications related to the stock-monitoring service. The information is not used for advertising or marketing.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS Consent

SMS notifications are sent only to users who have explicitly opted in to receive notifications from 5070 Stock Alert. Consent to receive SMS messages is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Opting Out

Recipients may opt out of SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP.
